# Behaviors

A **behavior** is ready-made movement or logic you add to an object in one tap: walk and jump with the joystick, drive like a car, follow the player, spin, float up and down… Add one with **Inspect → ＋ Add behavior**.

<Row>
  <Phone name="add-behavior-menu" caption="＋ Add behavior" />
  <Phone name="add-behavior-menu-end" caption="…more" />
</Row>

Each behavior card has a switch to turn it **on/off** and 🗑 to remove it. Events can do the same while playing (**Enable/disable behavior**) and change any setting (**Set behavior setting**); scripts use `entity.setBehaviorParam('platformer', 'speed', 8)`. In expressions: `Player.behavior("platformer", "speed")`.

## 🏃 Platformer {/* #platformer */}

<Side shot="behavior-platformer" crop>

Walk with the joystick (or WASD / arrows) and jump with a button. Needs a **dynamic Physics body** — adding the behavior adds one that doesn’t tip over, and a Collider.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speed** | 5 | Walking speed (m/s). |
| **Jump strength** | 7 | How high it jumps. |
| **Jump button** | jump | The touch button that jumps (see *Touch controls*). |
| **Turn to direction** | on | Face the way it moves. |
| **Camera relative** | on | “Up” on the joystick means *away from the camera*, not a fixed direction. |
| **Animate** | on | Play the model’s idle / walk / sprint / jump / fall clips automatically. |

</Side>

## 🕹️ Top-down movement {/* #topdown */}

<Side shot="behavior-topdown" crop>

Move in 8 directions on the ground with the joystick — no jumping. For top-down, twin-stick and rolling-ball games. Works with or without a Physics body (with one, it pushes and gets blocked by things).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speed** | 5 | Movement speed. |
| **Turn to direction** | on | Face the way it moves. |
| **Camera relative** | on | Joystick directions follow the camera. |
| **Animate** | on | Idle / walk / sprint clips automatically. |

</Side>

## 🏎️ Car {/* #car */}

<Side shot="behavior-car" crop>

Drive with the joystick: **up = gas**, **down = brake, then reverse**, **left/right = steer**. Real ray-cast vehicle physics with suspension. Needs a **dynamic Physics body**.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speed** | 15 | Top speed (m/s). |
| **Acceleration** | 10 | How fast it speeds up. |
| **Steering** | 30 | Largest steering angle (degrees). |
| **Grip** | 1 | Tyre grip. Lower = drifty, higher = sticky. |
| **Brake** | 1 | Braking strength. |
| **Wheels** | — | Which nodes of the car model are the wheels (they spin and steer). Tap to choose; mark the front ones. |

Wheels are found automatically in models whose parts are named “wheel…” (like the Kenney car kit). Otherwise pick them in **Wheels**; without wheels it drives on 4 invisible wheels at the corners.

</Side>

## 🎥 Camera follow {/* #camera-follow */}

<Side shot="behavior-cameraFollow" crop>

Put it **on the camera**: it smoothly follows a target.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Target** | Player | The object to follow. |
| **Offset X / Y / Z** | 0, 6, 10 | Where the camera stays, relative to the target (6 up, 10 behind). |
| **Smoothing** | 5 | Higher = snappier, lower = floatier. |
| **Look at target** | on | Keep pointing at the target. |
| **Stay behind** | off | Turn the offset with the target’s heading — a chase camera for cars and runners. |

</Side>

## 🔄 Rotate {/* #rotate */}

<Side shot="behavior-rotate" crop>

Spins constantly — coins, fans, windmills, planets.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speed X / Y / Z** | 0, 90, 0 | Degrees per second around each axis. |

</Side>

## ➤ Bullet {/* #bullet */}

<Side shot="behavior-bullet" crop>

Keeps moving forward (its −Z direction) — bullets, arrows, rockets, moving obstacles. Spawn it facing the right way (e.g. *Create object* at the gun, then *Set angle*).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speed** | 10 | Meters per second. |
| **Gravity** | off | Falls over time (arrows, cannonballs). |

</Side>

## 〰️ Wave (sine) {/* #sine */}

<Side shot="behavior-sine" crop>

Smoothly moves back and forth forever — floating pickups, bobbing boats, moving platforms, pulsing lights.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Property** | y | What waves: `x`, `y`, `z`, `angle`, `scale` or `opacity`. |
| **Magnitude** | 0.5 | How far (meters; degrees for angle). |
| **Period** | 2 | Seconds for one full wave. |

</Side>

## 🎯 Follow target {/* #follow */}

<Side shot="behavior-follow" crop>

Moves toward another object — chasing enemies, pets, homing missiles.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Target** | Player | What to follow. |
| **Speed** | 2 | Meters per second. |
| **Stop distance** | 1 | Stops this close. |
| **Look at** | on | Face the target. |
| **Animate** | on | Walk / idle clips automatically. |

</Side>

## 🗑️ Destroy when far {/* #destroy-offscreen */}

<Side shot="behavior-destroyOffscreen" crop>

Removes the object when it falls or goes too far — keeps games with many bullets or spawned obstacles fast.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kill Y** | −20 | Destroyed when it falls below this height. |
| **Max distance** | 200 | Destroyed this far from the camera (0 = never). |

</Side>

## ✋ Draggable {/* #draggable */}

<Side shot="behavior-draggable" crop>

The player can drag it with a finger — puzzles, sorting games, placing things.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Axis** | xz | `xz` = along the ground, `xy` = up/down and sideways (facing the camera), or a single axis `x`, `y`, `z`. |

</Side>
