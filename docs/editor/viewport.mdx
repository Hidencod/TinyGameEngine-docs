# The 3D view

The 3D view shows your scene. It is where you place things.

## Look around

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Drag** with one finger (on empty space) | Orbit around the scene |
| **Pinch** with two fingers | Zoom in and out |
| **Drag** with two fingers | Pan (slide the view) |
| **Double-tap** an object | Focus on it (the view centers on it) |
| Mouse: right/middle drag, wheel | Pan, zoom (when using the editor with a mouse) |

The grid shows the ground plane. One grid square is 1 meter.

## Select objects

- **Tap** an object to select it. Its name is highlighted in the Objects list and the [Inspector](./inspector) shows it.
- **Tap empty space** to deselect.
- **Long-press** an object to open its [object menu](./objects#object-menu) (rename, duplicate, delete…).

## Move, rotate and scale (the gizmo)

The selected object shows a **gizmo**. Drag its handles:

<Row>
  <Phone name="viewport-selected-move" caption="Move: drag an arrow" />
  <Phone name="viewport-rotate" caption="Rotate: drag a ring" />
  <Phone name="viewport-scale" caption="Scale: drag a handle" />
</Row>

The colors are the axes: **red = X** (left/right), **green = Y** (up/down), **blue = Z** (forward/back).

- In **Move** mode you can also drag the **object itself** to slide it along the ground.
- Every drag is one undo step.
- For exact values, type them in the Inspector’s **Transform** card.

### Snap

<Side shot="viewport-snap-on">

Turn on **Snap** to move in 0.5 m steps and rotate in 15° steps — handy for lining up walls and floors. A short message confirms it. Tap it again to turn it off.

</Side>

## Show colliders

<Side shot="viewport-colliders">

Physics uses **collider** shapes that can differ from what you see. Turn on **Colliders** to draw them as outlines. Useful when something falls through the floor or bumps into air. See [Physics](../world/physics).

</Side>

## Select several objects

<Side shot="viewport-multi">

<Legend items={[
  <>Tap <b>Multi</b>, then tap objects (in the view or the Objects list) to add or remove them.</>,
  <><b>Pivot</b> — turn and scale around the <b>middle</b> of the group, or each object around <b>its own</b> center.</>,
  <>The Objects tab shows how many are selected; tap it (<b>Done</b>) to finish.</>,
]} />

With a keyboard, hold **Shift** or **Ctrl** while tapping.

</Side>

<Side shot="inspector-multi">

The gizmo now moves, rotates and scales them **together**. The Inspector shows the group: tap a name to edit just that one, or **Duplicate all**, **Delete all**, **Clear selection**.

</Side>
