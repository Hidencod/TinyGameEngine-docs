# Windows and layout

On a computer (the desktop layout), the editor is made of **windows**, each a tab, as in Unity:

- **Objects**, **Files**, **Logic**, **Inspect** and **Scene settings**
- **Scene**, the 3D view
- **Game**, while the game plays in the editor
- **Timeline**, under the 3D view the first time
- every panel you open: an event sheet, a script, a shader, the Animator, Project settings…
- the windows of your [editor scripts](../logic/editor-scripts#windows)

<Crop name="docking-layout" width={900} caption="A rearranged layout: the event sheet beside the 3D view, Logic under it, an editor script's window under the Inspector" />

## Move a window

Drag its tab:

- **Onto another tab bar**: it joins that area, at the place you drop it.
- **Onto the edge of an area** (left, right, top or bottom): that area splits and the window gets the new half.
- **Onto the middle of an area**: it joins that area as its last tab.

A blue box shows where it will go before you let go. Press **Esc** to cancel.

<Crop name="docking-drop" width={900} caption="Dragging the event sheet to the right edge of the centre area: the blue box is the half it will take" />

An area whose last tab you drag away closes, and the areas beside it take its room.

## Floating windows

**Right-click a tab → Float**: the window comes out of the layout and floats over it, as an undocked window in Unity.

<Crop name="docking-float" width={900} caption="The Inspector floating over the 3D view" />

- **Drag the bar** at its top to move it, **the corner** at its bottom right to resize it.
- Drag other tabs onto it: a floating window has tabs and splits like any area.
- Drag its tab onto an area to dock it there, or **right-click → Dock** to put it back where it was.
- Clicking a floating window brings it to the front.

The tab's right-click menu also has **Maximize** and, for windows that close, **Close**.

## Resize, maximize, hide

- **Drag the gap** between two areas to resize them.
- **Ctrl + Space**: the 3D view's area fills the editor; again to go back. Right-click a tab → **Maximize** does it for any area.
- **N**: hide or show the Inspector's area.

## Which window gets the keys

Keys go to the area you last clicked in, as in Unity. With the event sheet focused, **Delete** deletes the selected event, not the selected object. Click the 3D view or the Objects list to use the editor's shortcuts there.

## Opening and closing

- Panels and the Game open **where they were last**. The first time, they open beside the 3D view.
- The Game opens when you press **Play** and closes when you stop. Its **×** stops the game.
- Objects, Files, Logic, Inspect, Scene settings and Scene can be moved but not closed. **Window** in the menu bar brings any of them to the front.

## Layouts

The arrangement is saved in this browser and comes back the next time you open the editor, in every project, floating windows included.

In **Window** in the menu bar, as Unity's Layout menu:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Layout: Default** | Lists on the left, the 3D view in the middle, the Inspector on the right |
| **Layout: Wide** | The 3D view across the top with the Inspector beside it, the lists along the bottom |
| **Layout: Tall** | The 3D view on the left, the lists over the Inspector on the right |
| **Save layout…** | Keep the current arrangement under a name; it is listed with the others |
| **Delete layout…** | Remove a saved one |
| **Reset layout** | Back to Default |

Open panels, the Game and the Timeline stay open when you switch.

On a phone the editor keeps its own layout: the 3D view on top and the five tabs below. Panels open full screen.
