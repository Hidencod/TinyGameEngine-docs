# Welcome to Tiny Game Engine

**Tiny Game Engine** lets you make real 3D games on an Android phone. You build the world with touch, make it work with **behaviors** and **event sheets** (no code needed), test it with one tap on **▶ Play**, and share it as an **Android app (APK)** or a **single HTML file**.

## What you can make

- 3D games of any kind: platformers, racing, endless runners, shooters, physics puzzles, collectathons.
- With characters and props from the built-in **free model library**, free **sounds** and **360° skies**.
- With real **physics** (gravity, collisions, bouncing, cars), **particles** (fire, smoke, confetti), **menus and HUD** (buttons, labels, health bars) and **touch controls** (joystick and buttons).

## How a game is made

1. **Objects** — everything in your game is an object: the player, the ground, coins, the camera, lights, labels.
2. **Components** — an object *is* something because of its components: a *Shape & look* makes it visible, a *Collider* lets it touch things, a *Physics body* gives it gravity.
3. **Behaviors** — ready-made movement: *Platformer* lets an object walk and jump with the joystick, *Camera follow* keeps the camera on it.
4. **Events** — the rules: *WHEN the Player collides with a Coin → DO destroy the Coin, add 1 to Score*.
5. **Play** — tap ▶ Play to try it; tap ■ to go back and change things.
