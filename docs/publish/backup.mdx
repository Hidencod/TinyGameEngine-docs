# Backup & move projects

Projects are saved **only on your phone**. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. A **backup** is a single `.tge` file with everything — scenes, events, scripts and assets.

## Make a backup

Either:

- In the editor: **⋮ → Backup project (.tge)**, or
- On the home screen: **⋮** on the project → **Backup (.tge)**.

The share sheet opens: save it to Files or Google Drive, or send it to yourself.

## Restore or move to another phone

On the home screen tap **⬇ Import .tge** and choose the file. It is added as a **new project** — it never replaces an existing one, so importing twice gives you two copies.

## Share a project with a friend

Send them the `.tge` file. They import it and can open, play and change your project — a great way to learn from each other or work on a game together.

:::tip[Good habits]
- Back up before big changes, or use **Duplicate** on the home screen to keep a copy.
- Keep backups outside the phone (Drive, email).
- Also back up your [APK signing key](./export-apk#signing-key) once.
:::
