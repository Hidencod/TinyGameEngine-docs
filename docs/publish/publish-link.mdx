# Publish with a link

**⋮ → Publish game (link)** uploads your game and gives you a link such as `play.jeevagames.com/g/k3x9a0qz`. Anyone who opens it plays your game in their browser, on a phone or a computer. They do not need Tiny Game Engine, an account or an install.

## Publish

1. Open **⋮ → Publish game (link)** (on a computer: **File → Publish game (link)**).
2. Tap **Publish game**. The editor builds the game and uploads it.
3. Tap **Share link** to send it by chat, mail or any app, or **Open** to try it.

## Change it later

Open the same menu for a published game:

- **Update** uploads the current version. The link stays the same, and the new version shows within a few minutes.
- **Share link** and **Open** work as before.
- **Unpublish** removes the game. Its link stops working.

## Show it in the gallery

The **Game gallery** (`play.jeevagames.com/gallery`) lists the newest games that makers chose to show. A game is **not** listed unless you ask.

- After publishing, tap **Show in gallery**, or open the menu later and tap **In gallery** to switch it on or off.
- The gallery picture is a screenshot of your scene, taken when you switch it on and again each time you **Update**. Open the scene you want people to see first. If the view isn't showing, the game's **app icon** (Project settings) is used, and without one the card shows the game's first letter.
- The newest game gets the big tile at the top, with a **Play** button.
- **Update** keeps the game listed and refreshes its title and picture.
- **Unpublish** takes it out of the gallery too.

## Reporting a game

Every game opened from `play.jeevagames.com` has a small **⚑** button in its bottom-left corner, and every gallery card has one too. Tap it, pick what is wrong (broken, adult or violent content, copied, or something else), and send. When **3 different people** report a game, it leaves the gallery on its own. Its link keeps working until it is taken down. Games that break the rules are removed.

## Limits

- Up to **5 published games** per device. At the limit, publishing another game asks which one to unpublish.
- Up to **50 MB** per game. Delete unused assets in the Assets tab, and prefer JPG/WebP images and MP3/OGG sounds, to keep it small.

## Who can change a game

There are no accounts. This device keeps a secret key, and whoever has that key can update or unpublish the games. Clearing the browser's site data (or using another browser or phone) loses the key: the games stay online but you can no longer change them from there. Use [Export as HTML](./export-html) to keep your own copy of the game, and [Backup](./backup) for the project.

## Good to know

- The game is the same file as **Export game (HTML)**: it runs entirely in the player's browser. Its saved data (scores, progress) stays on each player's own device.
- Anyone with the link can play. Do not publish a game you do not want others to see, or content you do not have the right to share.
- Games that break the rules or are reported are taken down.
