# Ball Arena

A small arena with twelve coins. Tap **Play online** and you join the room `ARENA`. Everyone else who taps it joins the same room, and you see their balls roll about while you collect coins. **Play alone** is the same game without a room.

It is a tour of the **multiplayer** blocks: a room with a code of your own, objects that every player sees, and telling your own ball from the others. **Everything is an event sheet and project data: there are no code scripts.**

**Controls:** the joystick rolls your ball (**WASD** or the **arrow keys** on a computer). To try it with a friend, open this page on two devices and tap **Play online** on both.

## Open it in the editor

1. Download **[ball-arena.tge](https://hidencod.github.io/tge-assets/showcase/ball-arena/ball-arena.tge)** (0.1 MB).
2. Open the editor: **[editor.jeevagames.com](https://editor.jeevagames.com)** on a computer, or the Android app.
3. On the home screen tap **Import .tge** and pick the file, then open **Ball Arena (imported)**.
4. Press **▶ Play**, or open **Logic → Main events** to read the rules.

## How it works

### One room for everyone

**Play online** runs **Create room** with the room code `"ARENA"`. The first player makes the room; anyone else who uses the same code joins it. That is a lobby without anybody typing a code. If it cannot connect, **On room failed** says why with `neterror()`.

### A ball for each player

Under **On room joined**, **Spawn for everyone** makes the `Ball` prefab at a random spot. Every player in the room sees it. The player who spawned it **drives** it; the others see a copy that glides to where it is. The `Ball` prefab has a **Net sync** component, which sends its position about ten times a second. When you leave, your ball goes for everyone.

**Play alone** runs the same **Spawn for everyone** with no room, and it just makes the ball.

### Only my ball listens to my finger

Every ball is called `Ball`, so the movement event has a second condition: **Ball is mine**. Only the ball you drive reads the joystick:

- **Every tick** and **Ball is mine** → **Set move input** `Ball` to the joystick.

The balls of other players do not run their behaviors on your device, so even without that check they would not move by themselves. The check is there so *your* joystick does not steer *their* ball in your own copy of the scene.

### Coins

When a ball touches a coin, the coin is destroyed on your screen. Under it, a sub-event with **Ball is mine** adds to your score. A friend's ball that rolls through a coin removes it from your screen but not from your count.

## Make it yours

- **A shared score:** send a `coin` message with **Send message** when you collect one, and let the host add them up. See [Multiplayer](../world/multiplayer).
- **Different colours:** each player sends the colour they picked with **Send message**, and everyone sets the colour of that player's ball.
- **A private room:** use **Create room** with no code, and show `netcode()` so a friend can **Join room** with it.
