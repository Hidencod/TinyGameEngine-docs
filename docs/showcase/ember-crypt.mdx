# Ember Crypt

The crypt is dark: only your lantern lights it. Walk into the four cold **braziers** to light them. Once all four burn, the gate at the end opens and you can walk out. Lava pits and a saw stand in the way.

It shows off [2D lights](../world/2d-lights) with [normal maps](../world/2d-lights#normal-maps):
- the lantern is a Spot light on a child of the hero, with **Normal Maps**, soft **Shadows** and a little **Volumetric** glow,
- the brick back wall, the stone floor, the braziers, the hero and a [Sprite Shape](../world/sprite-shape) rubble mound each have a normal map, so their bevels light up on the side facing the lantern,
- each brazier carries its own Light 2D, turned up by an event when you light it.

**The rules are an event sheet: there are no code scripts.** The hero, stone tiles and pickups are Kenney's *New Platformer Pack* (CC0). The bricks, braziers and all the normal maps are drawn by the project's code.

## Open it in the editor

1. Download **[ember-crypt.tge](https://hidencod.github.io/tge-assets/showcase/ember-crypt/ember-crypt.tge)** (470 KB).
2. Open the editor: **[editor.jeevagames.com](https://editor.jeevagames.com)** on a computer, or the Android app.
3. On the home screen tap **Import .tge** and pick the file, then open **Ember Crypt (imported)**.
4. Press **▶ Play**.

## How it works

### Lights

| Light | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Darkness* | Global | Dim blue, intensity 0.16: just enough to see the walls' outlines. |
| *Lantern* | Spot | A child of the hero at chest height, so it moves with the hero. Radius 7.5 m, **Shadows** (softness 0.45), **Normal Maps** (distance 1.1), **Volumetric** 0.1. |
| *Brazier* (×4) | Spot | On each brazier. Cold: radius 1.4 m, a faint ember glow. Lit: an event sets its **intensity** to 1.8 and its **outer radius** to 9. Normal Maps and Shadows on. |
| *Lava glow* (×2) | Freeform | An orange shape over each pit; its rim lights the walls above. |

The **Level** tilemap and the rubble mound are **Shadow Caster 2D**s, so the floor block, the ledges and the mound cast shadows from the lantern and the braziers.

![The rubble mound: a Sprite Shape with fill and edge normal maps](/img/showcase/ember-crypt-rubble.webp)

### Normal maps

Every lit picture has one:

| Picture | Where its normal map is set | How it was made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Crypt wall* (tilemap, 4 brick tiles) | Tilemap **Normal map** | From the bricks' height: raised bricks, worn edges, deep mortar, cracks. |
| *Level* (stone tiles) | Tilemap **Normal map** | From the tiles' brightness, laid out like the tile sheet. |
| *Rubble* (Sprite Shape) | **Fill normal map**, and a **Normal map** on each edge range | From brightness. The edges bend along the outline, and so do their bumps. |
| *Brazier* | Sprite **Normal map** | From the bowl's and stand's round shapes. |
| *Player* | Sprite **Normal map** | From brightness. |

Walk past a wall and watch the bricks: the edges facing the lantern light up, the far edges go dark, and it changes as you go.

### The rules

- **On clicked** `StartButton`: hide the start screen, `Started` = 1. Until then (and after you win) the hero doesn't take input.
- **Braziers:** touching a `#brazier` whose `lit` is 0 sets `lit` = 1, plays its *burn* animation, and uses **Set component setting** on its `light2d`: `intensity` to 1.8, `outerRadius` to 9. `Lit` goes up by 1, and its position is stored in `CheckX` and `CheckY` (the place you come back to).
- **The gate:** when `Lit` ≥ 4 (**Trigger once**), the `#gate` blocks sparkle and are destroyed, and the camera shakes.
- **The saw** (`#hazard`) slides back and forth with **Moving platform** and costs a heart. The **Health** behavior knocks you back and keeps you safe for 1.5 s.
- **Lava, or falling below the level**, costs a heart and sets `Respawn` = 1. The `Respawn` event puts the hero back at the last brazier lit.
- **On death** of the hero: `health` back to 3 and back to the last brazier.
- **Embers** (coins) and a **heart** on the top ledge are optional.
- **The way out** (`#goal`): `Won` = 1, confetti, and the win screen with the time and the embers.
- **The hint line** at the bottom says what to do next.

### Saved tests

The project has five [saved gameplay tests](../editor/tests) (**Tests** panel):
- **Start screen waits:** the hero doesn't move before Start.
- **Walking into a brazier lights it:** run right from the start; `Lit` = 1 and the checkpoint moves to it.
- **Lava costs a heart and brings you back.**
- **The gate stays shut until all four burn:** with `Lit` = 3, the hero stops at the gate.
- **Four braziers open the gate; the way out wins.**

They also run from the command line: `node tge-test.mjs ember-crypt.tge` (see [From the command line](../editor/tests#from-the-command-line)).

## Make it yours

- Pick the *Lantern* (under *Player* in the Objects list) and change its colour, radius or **Distance**: a low Distance makes the bumps stand out more.
- Switch **Normal Maps** off on the lantern to see the walls go flat.
- Give your own sprite a normal map: any normal-map maker's output works, laid out like the picture.
- Add a fifth brazier: copy one, and change the `4` in the gate event and the HUD text to `5`.
