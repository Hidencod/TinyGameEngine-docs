# Firefly Grove

Catch eight fireflies in a grove at night while two wisps hunt you. Listen to the music: the drums grow as a wisp comes closer, a darker bass and melody join when one is near, and when a wisp sees you the chase music takes over, right on the bar. Lose them and the grove music comes back, on the bar again. The fireflies pulse on every beat.

It shows the game music the engine makes. **Both songs were made in the beat maker, and everything else is an event sheet: there are no code scripts.**

**Controls:** the joystick moves the ball (**WASD** or the **arrow keys** on a computer). Play with the sound on.

## Open it in the editor

1. Download **[firefly-grove.tge](https://hidencod.github.io/tge-assets/showcase/firefly-grove/firefly-grove.tge)** (500 KB).
2. Open the editor: **[editor.jeevagames.com](https://editor.jeevagames.com)** on a computer, or the Android app.
3. On the home screen tap **Import .tge** and pick the file, then open **Firefly Grove (imported)**.
4. Press **▶ Play**, or open **Logic → Main events** to read the rules. **Files → music** has the two songs: tap one and choose **Edit beat** to open it in the beat maker.

## How it works

### The grove music, in layers

*Grove* is one beat with three parts (see [Layers](../world/sound#layers-adaptive-music)):

| Channels | Layer | When it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pad, Pluck | none | always |
| Kick, Hat, Shaker | **Drums** | louder as the nearest wisp comes closer |
| Bass, Lead | **Danger** | when a wisp is within 8 m |

Its playlist is a 1-bar intro and the main part twice, with **↻** set to bar 2: the intro plays once, then the rest loops.

**On start of scene** sets both layers to 0 and then plays the music, so it starts calm. Every tick the nearest wisp's distance goes into `Near`:

- **Pick nearest** `Wisp` to `Player` → **Set Near** to `distance(Player.x, Player.z, Wisp.x, Wisp.z)`.
- **Set music layer** `Drums` to `clamp((15 - Near) / 7, 0, 1)` (fade 0.3 s): silent beyond 15 m, full at 8 m.
- A sub-event: `Near < 8` → **Set music layer** `Danger` to 1; **Else** → to 0 (fade 2 s).

![A wisp near: the drums and the danger layer are in](/img/showcase/firefly-grove-danger.webp)

### The chase, on the bar

*Chase* is a second beat at the same tempo (120 BPM), so the two line up.

- A wisp within 5 m → **Play music** `Chase`, *In time with the music*: **On the next bar**. The grove keeps playing until that bar, then the chase starts on it. The alarm (**Play sound** `Zap`, **On the next beat**) waits for the beat.
- While chasing, the wisps **Move to position** `Player.x`, `Player.z` every tick.
- More than 9 m away for 3 seconds → **Play music** `Grove` on the next bar, and the wisps go back to wandering.

![Seen: the chase music](/img/showcase/firefly-grove-chase.webp)

### On the beat

- **On music beat** → the fireflies **Set size** to 0.85, then **Tween** size back to 0.5 in 0.35 s: a pulse on every beat.
- **On music bar** (every 2 bars), when no one is chasing → the wisps **Move to position** a random spot: they wander in time with the music.
- The last firefly → the win jingle (**Play sound** `Win`) **On the next bar**, the grove music back with full drums.

The text at the top right says what the music is doing, to watch it while you play.

### The night

A night sky, a little fog and **bloom** (glow). The fireflies and wisps have *Unlit* materials, so they glow. A warm point light is a child of the ball, a lantern that goes where it goes, and each wisp has a red one.

## Make it yours

- Open *Grove* with **Edit beat** and change the parts. The **🧅** chips at the top of the **Mixer** play it with layers turned off, as the game does. **Done** saves the layers too.
- Make a new layer: **🧅 → New layer…** on a channel, then a **Set music layer** with its name.
- A different chase: make another beat at 120 BPM and change `Chase` in the **Play music** action. Another tempo works too, but the change lands on the old song's bar, not on the new one's.
- Harder: more `Wisp` objects (duplicate one), a faster chase (3.4), or a shorter "lost them" time (3 s).
