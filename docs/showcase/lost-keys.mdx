# Lost Keys

Run from a sunny meadow, through stone caves full of lava, up to a snowy summit. Three locked walls block the way: find the **blue**, **yellow** and **red** keys to open them, and reach the red flag at the top.

It's one side-scrolling level that uses the [2D pieces](../world/2d-games) together:
- [tilemaps](../world/tilemaps) with [auto tiles](../world/tilemaps#auto-tiles), [animated tiles](../world/tilemaps#animated-tiles) (lava and torches), one-way platforms and a ladder,
- [sprites](../world/sprites) with animations, and a [polygon collider](../world/sprites#collision) for the snow slope,
- the [Platformer](../behaviors#platformer), [Moving platform](../behaviors#moving-platform), [Wave](../behaviors#sine), [Parallax](../behaviors#parallax) and [Health](../behaviors#health) behaviors.
- [2D lights](../world/2d-lights): dark caves lit by torches with soft shadows and glowing lava.

**The rules are an event sheet: there are no code scripts.** The pictures are Kenney's *New Platformer Pack* (CC0).

**Controls:** press **Start**. The joystick runs (and climbs on the ladder), the **A** button jumps. On a computer: **A / D** or the **arrow keys** to run, **Space** to jump, **W / S** on the ladder.

## How it works

### The level: three tilemaps

The level is drawn with one tile sheet (64 px = 1 m) on three tilemaps:

| Tilemap | Order | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Level* | 0 | The ground, walls and platforms: **Solid**. Grass, stone and snow are **auto tiles** (block), so each painted cell picks its edge or corner piece. The thin platforms are **One-way**: you jump up through them and land on top. The ladder is **Climb**. |
| *Decorations* | 2 | Bushes, mushrooms, rocks, fences, signs and torches: no collider, drawn in front. |
| *Cave wall* | −5 | The dark stone behind the caves: no collider, drawn behind everything. |

The lava's top row and the torches are **animated tiles**: two frames each.

![The caves: lava, torches and the lift](/img/showcase/lost-keys-caves.webp)

### The meadow: a spring and a high ledge

The blue key sits on a ledge too high to jump to. The spring beside it throws the hero up: when the hero lands on it (**On collision** with `#spring` while falling), the event sets the hero's upward speed to 11.2 and plays the spring's *out* animation.

![The spring throws the hero up to the blue key](/img/showcase/lost-keys-spring.webp)

### The caves: a lift, a ladder and a bee

- **The lift** is a small tilemap with the **Moving platform** behavior (6 m, then back). The lava pit is too wide to jump, so you ride it across.
- **The ladder** leads to the yellow key on top of a pillar. Tiles set to **Climb** let a Platformer hero with climbing on go up and down them. The ladder reaches one tile above the pillar, so you can step off onto it.
- **The bee** goes up and down with the **Wave** behavior: pass under it when it's high.

![The ladder up to the yellow key](/img/showcase/lost-keys-ladder.webp)

### The summit: a polygon collider and a saw

The snow slope up from the caves is a sprite, not tiles: its **Collision** shape in the sprite editor is a triangle, so the hero walks up it smoothly. The Platformer climbs slopes up to its **Max slope** (50°).

The saw slides along the ground with **Moving platform**. The red key is at the top of three snow steps.

![The summit: snow steps up to the red key](/img/showcase/lost-keys-summit.webp)

### The camera and the backdrop

The camera is orthographic (size 5) with **Camera follow**. Its limits are the level's edges, so it never shows past them. Behind the level, two hill pictures and the clouds have the **Parallax** behavior: each moves at its own share of the camera's speed, and **Wrap width** repeats them along the whole level.

### Lights

The caves are lit with [2D lights](../world/2d-lights). Once a scene has a Light 2D, sprites and tilemaps show only the light of 2D lights, so the outside needs light too:
- **Daylight**: a white Freeform light, intensity 1, whose shape is everything except the caves. Its soft rim (Falloff 2 m) makes the cave mouths and roof fade into the dark.
- **Cave air**: a dim blue Freeform light over the caves, so the dark parts stay readable.
- **Torch light**: a warm Spot light on each torch, with **Shadows** and a little **Shadow Softness**; the **Level** tilemap is a **Shadow Caster 2D**, so the pillar and the walls cast shadows.
- **Lava glow**: an orange Freeform light over each lava pit, its rim lighting the walls above.

### The rules

- **On clicked** `StartButton`: hide the start screen, `Started` = 1. Until then (and after you win) the hero doesn't take input.
- **Keys:** touching `#keyblue` sets `HasBlue` = 1. When `HasBlue` = 1 (**Trigger once**), the `#lockblue` blocks sparkle and are destroyed, and the key icon shows at the top right. The same for yellow and red.
- **Checkpoints:** touching a `#checkpoint` whose `reached` is 0 raises its flag and stores its position in `CheckX` and `CheckY`.
- **Snails** walk between their own `minX` and `maxX`, turning round on **On arrived**. Land on one from above to defeat it (2 coins); touching it any other way costs a heart. The **Health** behavior's knockback pushes you away, and its 1.5 s invincibility stops a second hit.
- **The bee and the saw** (`#hazard`) cost a heart.
- **Lava, or falling below the level**, costs a heart and sets `Respawn` = 1. The `Respawn` event puts the hero back at the last checkpoint.
- **On death** of the hero: `health` back to 3 (setting it above 0 brings a dead object back) and back to the checkpoint.
- **The red flag** (`#goal`): `Won` = 1, confetti, and the win screen with the time and the coins.
- **The hint line** at the bottom says where the next key is.

### Saved tests

The project has five [saved gameplay tests](../editor/tests) (**Tests** panel):
- **Start screen waits:** the hero doesn't move before Start.
- **Coins on the way, the gap costs a heart:** run right from the start into the first gap.
- **The blue wall stops you without its key.**
- **Spring up to the blue key opens the wall.**
- **Red key and the flag win.**

They also run from the command line: `node tge-test.mjs lost-keys.tge` (see [From the command line](../editor/tests#from-the-command-line)).

## Make it yours

- Paint more level in the 2D view: pick a grass, stone or snow auto tile and the edges follow.
- Give the lift a longer **Move X**, or turn the saw into a lift.
- Add a fourth key: a key sprite with its own colour tag, a column of locks with theirs, and three events copied from the blue ones.
- Change the hero's **Jump strength** and **Speed** in its Platformer behavior, and check the jumps still work with **Run tests**.
