# Editable mesh

An **editable mesh** is a shape you change right in the 3D view: pick its corners (vertices), edges or faces, then move, turn or scale them with the transform handles, or extrude, inset and subdivide them. Use it to block out levels, rooms, ramps and props without leaving the editor. It is Unity's ProBuilder.

![An editable mesh in the 3D view: two faces picked in orange with the move handles on them, and the Inspector's Edit mesh controls](/img/shots/editable-mesh.webp)

## Make one

Add any shape (a cube, cylinder, plane …), then on its **Shape & look** card set **Shape → Editable mesh**. The shape stays as it was, now made of faces you can edit: a cube is 8 corners and 6 squares, a cylinder keeps its round ends as single faces.

## Edit it

1. Press **Edit mesh**. The object's edges show over it.
2. Under **Select**, choose what taps pick: **Vertex**, **Edge** or **Face**.
3. Tap to pick one. **Shift + click** (or **Select several** on a phone) adds more or takes one away. A tap on nothing clears the picks.
4. The transform handles sit on the picks: the **Move**, **Rotate** and **Scale** tools work on them as they do on objects. **Snap** moves in steps of 0.25 m and turns in steps of 15°.
5. Press **Done** to go back to editing objects.

Corners hidden behind the mesh are drawn faintly, and taps only pick what you can see.

**Shift + drag** on a face's move arrow extrudes the faces first and then moves the new ones out, as in ProBuilder.

Every change is one undo step.

### Keyboard (like Blender's edit mode)

With the mouse over the 3D view:

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Tab** | Edit mesh / Done (on a selected editable mesh). |
| **1 · 2 · 3** | Pick vertices · edges · faces. |
| **G · R · S** | Move · rotate · scale the picks with the mouse, no handle needed. |
| then **X · Y · Z** | Lock to that world axis (**Shift + X / Y / Z**: to the plane without it). The same key again locks to the object's own axis, a third time lets go. |
| then type a number | An exact amount: metres, degrees or times (**-** flips it). |
| hold **Ctrl** · **Shift** | Snap (0.25 m, 15°, 0.25×) · move a tenth as far, for fine work. |
| **Click** or **Enter** · **Esc** or **right-click** | Keep · put it back as it was. |
| **E** | Extrude the picked faces (or open border edges) and pull the copy out along the faces' normal; type a number for an exact length. **X / Y / Z** pulls it another way. |
| **I** | Inset the picked faces (by **Inset (m)**). |
| **Alt + click** | Pick a whole loop: in Vertex and Edge mode the edge loop through the edge you click near (it runs on through corners where four four-sided faces meet); in Face mode the ring of faces across the edge you click near. **Shift + Alt + click** adds it. |
| **Ctrl + R** | Loop cut through the edge under the mouse: **Loop cuts** new loops around the ring of four-sided faces it crosses. |
| **Ctrl + B** | Bevel the picked edges by **Bevel (m)**. |
| **A · Alt+A · Ctrl+I** | Pick everything · nothing · the rest. |
| **X** or **Delete** | Delete the picks. |

## Operations

| Picking | Button | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faces | **Extrude** | Pushes the faces out along their direction by **Extrude (m)**, with walls joining them to the rest. Faces that touch stay joined. |
| Faces | **Inset** | A smaller copy of each face inside it, **Inset (m)** in from its sides, joined by a ring of faces. Inset, then extrude, makes a window or a raised panel. |
| Faces | **Subdivide** | Splits each face from the middle of its sides (a square becomes four). |
| Faces | **Flip** | Turns the faces to show the other side. |
| Edges | **Extrude** | Open border edges only: a new face hangs off each edge, **Extrude (m)** out. |
| Edges | **Loop cut** | New edge loops (**Loop cuts** of them) across the ring of four-sided faces through the first picked edge, splitting each into strips. The new loops are picked. |
| Edges | **Bevel** | Each picked edge becomes a strip **Bevel (m)** wide, taking off the hard edge. **Bevel segments** 1 gives a flat slant; more give a rounded edge. Edges that meet are fine: pick every edge of a box (**Ctrl+A**) for a rounded box, with smooth corner patches where three bevels meet. Edges on an open border are skipped. |
| Edges | **Split** | A new corner in the middle of each edge. |
| Edges | **Bridge** | Two open border edges: a face across the gap between them. |
| Vertices | **Merge** | Joins the picked corners into one at their middle. |
| Vertices | **Weld** | Joins every pair of corners closer than 1 cm (after moving corners onto each other). |
| Any | **Delete** (or the **Delete** key) | Removes what is picked, and the faces that use it. |
| Any | **Select all** | Picks everything of the current kind. |
| Any | **Conform normals** | Turns faces that show the wrong side to match their neighbours. |
| Any | **Center pivot** | Moves the object's origin to the middle of the mesh (the mesh stays where it was). |

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Vertices · edges · faces** | How many the mesh has (and, in brackets, how many are picked). |
| **Smooth** | Smooth shading: faces meeting at a soft angle (under 60°) blend into each other. Off: every face is flat. |
| **UV scale** | How many times the texture repeats per metre. Textures are laid on each face from the side it faces, so they line up across faces and keep their size when you stretch the mesh. |

The material, shadows and baked lighting settings below work as on any shape.

## Face settings

In **Face** mode, the picked faces get settings of their own (like ProBuilder's per-face materials, vertex colours and UV settings). They show under the operations once a face is picked, and take the values of the first face picked.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Face material** | A shared material (saved from any object with **Material → Save this as a material…**) for these faces. **Object material** puts them back on the object's own material. One mesh can use as many materials as you like. |
| **Select same material** | Picks every face that uses the same material as the picked ones. |
| **Face color** | A colour painted on the faces, mixed with their material (white = none). **Clear** removes it. |
| **UV offset** | Moves the texture across the faces (in repeats, along u and v). |
| **UV rotation** | Turns the texture on the faces (degrees). |
| **UV tiling** | How many times the texture repeats on the faces, on top of **UV scale** (u and v). **Reset UVs** puts all three back. |

Face settings stay with their faces when you extrude, inset or subdivide them: new faces take the settings of the face they came from. Deleting a shared material puts its faces back on the object's material.

## UV Editor

**Window → UV Editor** (or **UV Editor** in Face settings) opens a 2D view of the picked faces' texture coordinates (every face when none are picked), drawn over the face's texture. The bright square is the texture once; the faint copies around it show where it repeats.

- **Drag a corner** to move it on the texture. Corners of the same mesh corner at the same spot move together, so faces that share an edge stay joined. **Shift + click** adds corners to the picks.
- **Drag inside a face** to move the whole face.
- Hold **Ctrl** while dragging to move in sixteenths.
- **Drag empty space** to pan, the **mouse wheel** to zoom; **Frame** puts the texture back in view.
- **90°**, **Flip U**, **Flip V** and **Fit** (scale to fill the texture square) act on the picked corners, or on all shown faces when fewer than two points are picked.
- **Unwrap** (Blender's Smart UV Project): lays the faces out flat for you. Touching faces that face roughly the same way (within 66°) become one piece, each piece is turned to take the least room, and all pieces are packed into the texture square at one size, so the texture looks equally sharp everywhere. Start here, then drag pieces where you want them.
- **Project from view**: the faces get the texture as the scene view sees them, like a slide projector at the camera. Good for a decal-like picture on a front.
- **Reset** gives the faces back their projected UVs.

Once a face's corners are moved here, it keeps its own UVs: moving the mesh's corners no longer slides the texture across it, and the UV offset / rotation / tiling rows no longer apply. A face whose corner count changes (a loop cut or bevel through it) goes back to projected UVs.

## Collider

A collider set to **Auto** or **Mesh** follows the editable mesh's real faces, so you can walk up a ramp or through an arch you shaped. On a moving rigidbody (or with **Convex**) it becomes a hull around the faces.
