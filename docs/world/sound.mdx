## Get sounds

<Side shot="sounds-library">

**Assets → 🔊 Free sounds** opens 27 game sounds by Kenney (CC0, free for any game), grouped as **Rewards** (Coin, PowerUp, Bonus, Win, Lose), **Actions** (Jump, Laser, Zap…), **Hits** (Explosion, Punch, GlassBreak…), menu clicks and engine hums.

- Tap **▶** to listen.
- **＋ Add** puts it in your project; **Use** means it is already there.

</Side>


**Your own sounds:** **Assets → ⬆ Import** an MP3, OGG, WAV or M4A. Preview any sound from its menu in the Assets tab (**Preview sound**).

## Play a sound from an event

The simplest way: **Add action → Audio → Play sound**, choose the sound, a **volume** and whether it **loops**. It can also play through a **mixer group**, at another **pitch** (1 = normal, 1.2 = a little higher and faster; a small random pitch like `random(0.9, 1.1)` keeps repeated sounds from sounding the same) and **fade in**. **Stop sound** stops one sound or all of them, with an optional **fade out**. **Is sound playing** checks whether one is still going.

Example — a coin sound: *WHEN Player collides with Coin → Audio: Play sound `Coin`*.

## The Sound component

For a sound that belongs to an object — an engine hum on a car, a waterfall, background music — add the **Sound** component:

<Side shot="card-audio" crop>

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sound file** | The sound asset (or pick a free one). |
| **Play on start** | Starts when the scene starts. |
| **Loop** | Repeats forever. |
| **Volume** | 0–1. |
| **Pitch** | 1 = normal; 2 = an octave up and twice as fast. |
| **Mixer group** | The group of the [audio mixer](#the-audio-mixer) it plays through (Master = none). |
| **3D sound** | Quieter when the object is far from the camera, and from the side it is on. |
| **Full volume within** | (3D) Closer than this, it plays at full volume. |
| **Heard up to** | (3D) How far it carries. |
| **Fade with distance** | (3D) *Natural* fades like real sound and never quite goes silent; *Linear* reaches silence at **Heard up to**. |

Actions **Play its sound** / **Stop its sound** (with a fade out) control it.

</Side>

:::info[Sound on phones]
Browsers only allow sound after the player touches the screen. Exported games show a *Tap to play* screen that unlocks sound; in the editor the ▶ Play tap does it.
:::

## Music

**Play music** plays one track at a time: starting another **cross-fades** from the one playing over the time you give (1 s by default), and asking for the track that is already playing only changes its volume. **Stop music** fades it out; **Is music playing** checks it. Use it for level music, a menu theme, or a boss fight that takes over.

## The audio mixer

**Project settings → Audio mixer** holds the game's **groups**, like Unity's Audio Mixer: for example *Music*, *SFX* and *Voices*. A sound plays through the group you pick (in **Play sound**, **Play music** or the Sound component); a group sets the volume of everything in it.

| Group setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What sounds and events call it. Renaming it renames it on every Sound component. |
| **On** | Off mutes the group. |
| **Volume** | 0–1, under the master volume. |
| **Low-pass** | Muffles the group (in Hz; 0 = off). 500–800 sounds like being under water or behind a wall. |

While playing:

| Block | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set group volume** | A group's volume, faded over the time you give. `Master` is the master volume. An options menu's music slider is one of these. |
| **Mute group** | Silence a group, or bring it back. |
| **Set group low-pass** | Muffle a group (fade it in when the player dives, out when they come up). |
| **Pause all sounds** / **Resume all sounds** | For a pause menu: everything stops where it is and carries on later. |

## In scripts

```js
const hit = audio.play('Punch', { group: 'SFX', pitch: random(0.9, 1.1) })
const engine = audio.play('Engine', { loop: true, fadeIn: 0.5 })
engine.pitch = 0.8 + speed / 20        // change it while it plays
engine.stop(1)                          // fade out over 1 s

audio.playMusic('Boss', { fade: 2 })     // cross-fade from the level music
audio.setGroupVolume('Music', 0.3, 1)    // duck the music for a cutscene
audio.setGroupLowpass('SFX', 600, 0.3)   // under water
audio.pauseAll()
```

`audio.play()` returns the sound: `volume`, `pitch`, `playing`, `stop(fadeOut)` and `fadeTo(volume, seconds)`. Also `audio.stop(tag, fadeOut)`, `audio.isPlaying(tag)`, `audio.music`, `audio.stopMusic(fade)`, `audio.getGroupVolume(group)`, `audio.setGroupMuted(group, muted)`, `audio.resumeAll()`, and `entity.stopSound(fadeOut)`.
