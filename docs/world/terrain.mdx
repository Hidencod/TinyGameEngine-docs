# Terrain

<Art name="penguin" size={95} />

A **terrain** is ground with hills: a square of land you shape by dragging on it, a bit like sculpting clay. It paints itself — grass where it's flat, rock on steep slopes, snow up high if you want, each with its own surface texture — and everything that walks, rolls or drives follows its hills.

## Add a terrain

**Objects → ＋ Add → Terrain (hills)**. It starts with gentle random hills, 40 × 40 m, centred in the scene, and the camera zooms out to show all of it.

<Row>
  <Phone name="terrain-view" caption="A new terrain" />
</Row>

New projects also have a flat **Ground**; delete it if the terrain replaces it, or keep it as a floor around the hills.

## Shape it

<Side shot="card-terrain" crop>

Select the terrain, open **Inspect** and tap **🖌️ Sculpt**. Then drag on the terrain in the 3D view:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Raise** | Pushes the ground up under the brush. Holding still keeps raising. |
| **Lower** | Digs it down (valleys, lakes, paths). |
| **Smooth** | Rounds off bumps and sharp edges. |
| **Flatten** | Levels the ground to the height where the drag started — roads, building plots, a flat arena on a hill. |
| **🎨 Paint** | Paints a surface onto the ground with a soft edge: a dirt path, a sandy beach, a cobbled road, rock or snow patches, more grass. Pick a surface (**Grass, Rock, Dirt, Snow, Stones, Sand**), each with its own texture, and change its **Tint** for any color (dark grass, red rock…). **Auto** erases paint back to the automatic look. |
| **🌳 Trees** | Sprinkles copies of a 3D model over the ground under the brush — a forest, rocks, bushes. See below. |
| **🌿 Details** | Paints grass, flowers and small stones that the terrain draws itself, thousands at a time, swaying in the wind (Unity's Paint Details). See below. |
| **🕳 Holes** | Cuts holes in the ground — cave mouths, pits, a well. See below. |

**Brush size** is the radius in meters; **Strength** is how fast it works. A yellow ring shows where the brush is. Each drag is one step of **Undo**.

While sculpting, one finger (or the left mouse button) sculpts, two fingers (or the right mouse button) move and zoom the camera. Tap **Stop sculpting** to turn the camera again.

</Side>

Painting changes only the colors, not the shape, and stays when you reshape or change **Detail**. **🧽 Clear paint** removes all of it.

### Paint with your own images {/* #images */}

Next to the built-in surfaces, **＋ Image** adds a surface made from a picture: one already in your **Assets**, a new one from your phone's gallery or your computer (**Import image**), or one of the **Free textures** (lawn, forest floor, gravel, sand, rock, cobblestones, stone slabs, wood planks, blue tiles). It gets its own button with the picture on it; paint with it like any other surface.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tint** | White keeps the picture's own colors; any other color tints it. |
| **Image size** | How many meters one copy of the picture covers before it repeats. |
| **🗑 Remove image** | Takes it off the list; ground painted with it turns to dirt (Undo brings it back). |

A terrain can have up to **4** image surfaces. Use **seamless** textures (made to repeat without visible edges) — the free ones are. A normal photo shows a seam every time it repeats. Big photos are scaled down to 2048 pixels when you import them, so they stay light on phones.

### Scatter trees and rocks {/* #scatter */}

With **🌳 Trees**, pick a **Model** (any 3D model in the project — add one first from **Objects → ＋ Add → Free models library…**) and drag over the terrain. Copies stand on the ground with a random turn and size, and skip slopes too steep to stand on.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Density** | How many per 100 m². Going over the same spot again only fills gaps — it never piles them up. |
| **Size from / to** | Each copy gets a random size in this range. |
| **Solid** | Copies get a Collider and block the player and [pathfinding](../behaviors#follow) — on for trees and rocks, off for grass and flowers. |
| **Remove** | The brush removes scattered copies instead of adding them. **🗑 Remove all scattered** clears them all. |

Scattered copies are ordinary objects inside the terrain (they move with it) and are tagged `#scattered`. The terrain's row in the Objects list folds when you scatter, so they don't flood it — tap its ▸ to see them. Each drag is one Undo step. Put `#scattered` in the camera's **Fade tag** and turn on **Fade in way** ([Camera follow](../behaviors#camera-follow)) so trees that block the view turn see-through.

:::tip
Every copy is a real object: a few hundred are fine, thousands slow phones down. For grass and flowers use **🌿 Details** instead.
:::

### Paint grass and flowers {/* #details */}

**🌿 Details** paints small things that cover the ground in great numbers: grass, flowers, pebbles. They are not objects — the terrain places and draws them itself, a whole patch at a time, so tens of thousands cost about as much as a few objects. Each kind is a **layer**:

| Add | What it draws |
| --- | --- |
| **＋ Grass** | Tufts of grass blades, no picture needed. |
| **＋ Image** | Two crossed cards showing a picture with see-through parts (flowers, ferns, weeds). |
| **＋ Model** | Copies of a 3D model (stones, mushrooms, small plants). |

<Row>
  <Phone name="card-terrain-details" caption="The Details tool with a grass layer" />
  <Phone name="terrain-grass" caption="Grass painted over the hills" />
</Row>

Tap a layer's button to paint with it and drag over the terrain. Going over a spot again fills it up to the **Target strength**; **Erase** removes it again.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Picture / Model** | What an Image or Model layer shows. |
| **Target strength** | How full the brush paints (1 = the full density, 0.3 = sparse). |
| **Density** | Most copies per square meter where it is painted fully. |
| **Height / Width** | Each copy gets a random size between Min and Max (meters for grass and images; a scale for models). |
| **Healthy color / Dry color** | Copies get colors between the two, in patches a few meters across, like real grass. Images and models are tinted by it (white keeps their own colors). |
| **Sway** | Bends in the wind. |
| **🧽 Clear / 🗑 Remove** | Clears the layer's painting / removes the layer. |
| **Detail distance** | How far from the camera details are drawn (default 60 m). Further away they thin out and shrink, so there is no hard edge. |
| **Wind speed / Wind size / Bending** | The waves that run over the grass: how fast, how wide, how far it bends. A [Wind Zone](particles#wind-zones) makes it blow harder and turns it while playing. |

Details sit on the ground, never in a hole, and follow the hills when you reshape them. They have no collider: players walk through grass. On phones, the quality levels draw fewer of them (**Terrain detail density** in Project settings → Graphics: Medium draws 60 %, Low 35 %); scripts change it with `game.quality.detailDensity`.

### Cut holes {/* #holes */}

**🕳 Holes** cuts the ground away under the brush, square by square: the terrain, its collider and its grass are gone there, so things fall through. Put a cave, a tunnel or a pit made of other objects below it. Turn on **Fill** to close holes again; **🗑 Fill all holes** closes every one. Pathfinding does not walk over holes.

**⛰️ Make hills** replaces the terrain with new random hills up to **Hill height** meters (Undo brings the old ones back); **Flatten all** makes it flat again.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Size** | Width and depth in meters. Making it bigger stretches the same hills. |
| **Detail** | Squares per side: Low (32), Medium (64) or High (128). More detail = smaller bumps possible, but more work for the phone. Changing it keeps the shape, the paint, the details and the holes. Holes are cut a square at a time, so High makes finer holes. |
| **Grass / Rock / Snow** | The colors it paints with. Rock goes on steep slopes. |
| **Snow above** | Snow covers the ground above this height (0 = no snow). |
| **Textures** | Automatic surface detail on the ground you haven't painted: grass blades and clumps, rough rock on steep slopes, soft snow. The colors still decide the look. Switch it off for a plain, raw-colored terrain — everything you **paint** (Stones, Sand…, and your own images) still shows its texture, so you can texture just the parts you want. |
| **Texture size** | How many meters one repeat of the detail covers (default 4). Smaller = finer detail. |

## Physics

A terrain comes with a **Collider** (shape *Auto*), which follows the hills exactly: players walk up them, balls roll down, cars drive over them. Don't give it a dynamic Rigid body — a terrain stays put.

[Pathfinding](../behaviors#follow) treats a terrain as ground to walk on, not as a wall. Steep slopes are still climbed only as far as the physics allows.

:::tip
Keep **Detail** at Medium unless you need small bumps: a High terrain has four times as many triangles. One big terrain is cheaper than many small ones.
:::
